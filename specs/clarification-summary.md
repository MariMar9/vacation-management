# Clarification Summary

**Scope:** Review of all six current feature specifications, their 24 user stories, the full requirements baseline, and the current RFP analysis. The feature specs are [F-001](001-vacation-request-lifecycle/requirements.md), [F-002](002-approval-and-delegation/requirements.md), [F-003](003-demand-and-capacity-visibility/requirements.md), [F-004](004-notifications-and-escalation/requirements.md), [F-005](005-organizational-data-and-integrations/requirements.md), and [F-006](006-application-entry-and-access/requirements.md); sources are the [Requirements Baseline](../origin/Requirements-Baseline.md) and [RFP analysis](../origin/RFP-Vacation-Management.md).

**Source status:** The baseline says Draft and reports no formal approval evidence; the RFP analysis says “Draft - source requirements require clarification.” The feature specs also preserve that status. This summary does not treat any source requirement, proposal, or status as approved.

**Test-project disposition:** The user authorized `MariMar9` as the single decision authority for this synthetic-data test workspace. Q-001–Q-027 have test-only dispositions in [the feature decision register](test-project-feature-decisions.md), linked to the [T001/T002 approval record](../.boltf/memory/test-project-approval.md). The source questions below remain open for real-world, external-system, regulated-data, or production use; their original wording is retained as provenance.

**Impact key:** `Blocker` means the answer is needed to define or verify core scope, authority, or data exchange; `High` means it materially affects workflow correctness, access, operations, or acceptance; `Moderate` means it affects a bounded capability or quality criterion. Each row gives the rationale. Category tags use only `contradiction`, `actor`, `term`, `threshold`, `data ownership`, `integration`, `testability`, and `governance`.

Questions are intentionally unanswered. Feature/US and baseline references identify the affected material; no stakeholder owner is presumed.
