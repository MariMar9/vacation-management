# Vacation Management Requirements Baseline

> **Baseline status:** Draft - the source RFP is marked Draft; no formal approval evidence was found.  
> **Prepared:** 2026-10-07  
> **Scope:** Requirements evidenced by the current RFP and its discovery sources. This baseline does not approve source proposals or fill gaps with assumed values.

## Status and Traceability Rules

- **Confirmed** means an unambiguous need or behavior was explicitly stated in the discovery record. It does not mean that the RFP or this baseline has been formally approved.
- **Clarification needed** means the source is conflicting, incomplete, or too subjective to accept as written.
- **Draft** means the item is proposed, optional, future-stage, or otherwise not established as a current committed requirement.
- Acceptance evidence is an observable way to verify the statement. Where the source supplies no testable condition, the evidence is explicitly marked as pending stakeholder definition.
- Dependencies use baseline identifiers where applicable. “None stated” means no dependency was stated in the sources, not that none exists.

## Cross-Cutting Clarifications
